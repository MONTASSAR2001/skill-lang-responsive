Plan every sprint in one workspace

Fluxboard brings your backlog, your boards and your deployments together. Create a workspace, invite your team and ship faster.

Workspaces and boards

A workspace holds all your boards. Each board has columns, and each column holds cards. Move a card from the backlog to the sprint board with a single drag. Every workspace has its own members, roles and billing.

Roles and seats

Each seat can be an admin, an editor or a viewer. Admins manage the workspace, editors update boards, and viewers can read but not change anything. You pay per seat, and an unused seat can be reassigned at any time.

Sprints and the backlog

Plan a sprint by dragging cards from the backlog into the sprint. When the sprint ends, unfinished cards return to the backlog automatically. Review the sprint report to see velocity.

Deploy and roll back

Connect a repository and Fluxboard Cloud will deploy every merge request that passes review. If something breaks, roll back with one click. Each deployment is linked to its cards, so you always know what shipped.

Integrations

Use a webhook to notify your team in chat. Authenticate with an API key or with OAuth. Enterprise workspaces also support SSO. Kanban teams can switch off sprints entirely and use the board as a continuous flow.
